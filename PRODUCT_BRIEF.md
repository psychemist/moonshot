# Learning Graph

**Project planning date:** 7 October 2026 · **Build week:** 8–14 October 2026  
**Showcase:** Moonshot by TechCabal, Lagos, 28–29 October 2026 (dates supplied in the brief).

**Who it serves.** Ada is an illustrative Lagos-based self-directed learner modeled on the local PRD: she knows Python, JavaScript, and basic calculus, studies 12 hours a week, and wants practical systems skills toward a longer-term robotics ambition. Her name, location, and profile are assumptions, not an interviewed person's biography.

**The problem.** Ada needs to know what to learn next without completing every related book first. The PRD's concrete assembler example shows the trap: a symbol-table task exposes gaps in C pointers and memory, and the learner delays the project while pursuing more prerequisite study. This is an illustrative PRD scenario, not a documented real-user episode; validate it with a learner before claiming impact.

**What the product does today.** Learning Graph turns one supported goal—build a Hack assembler in C—plus prior knowledge and weekly study hours into a complete curated map and one task within the weekly budget. Twelve concepts and the project are inspectable through a graph or accessible list, with eight resource entries. Each step includes an exercise, expected output, provisional estimate, resources, and explanation. Known concepts stay visible as self-reported. Optional small steps, focus view, pause controls, and locally saved restart notes support starting and returning; effectiveness needs learner validation. A local MCP server exposes the same planner and resources; the learning-coach skill guides level-appropriate explanations and hints through a configured assistant. The assembler is a systems project, not a mandatory robotics prerequisite.

**Why now.** The approaching showcase and one week working mostly with Codex make a focused proof of concept feasible. Whether this helps learners persist is a hypothesis to test, not an established market fact.

**The demo's wow moment.** Enter “build an assembler in C,” see the map and first task, then change prior C knowledge to change the recommendation. A connected coach can request the same plan conversationally, grounded in the planner's budget and sources.

**Next and out of scope.** Saved progress, reminders, and complete weekly scheduling are next. Universal curricula, automated mastery assessment, course generation, social features, accounts, hardware projects, and unrestricted tutoring are outside this prototype.
